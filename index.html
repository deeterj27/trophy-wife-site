How To Use

The five-minute finishing school.

Finishing salt is embarrassingly easy to use. Here's the whole curriculum.

Step 01

The Pinch-and-Finish

Cook. Plate. Pinch from 10 inches above. Eat. Accept compliments. Professional chefs do this. Now you do too.

Step 02

The Egg Upgrade

Garlic Butter makes a fried egg taste like culinary school. Spicy Citrus makes avocado toast taste like $22. Cinnamon Sugar turns French toast into a personality trait.

Step 03

The Sweet-Savory Move

Cinnamon Sugar finishing salt on vanilla ice cream will make your guests lose their minds in a quiet, civilized way. This is not a party trick. This is a lifestyle.

Step 04

The Bag Move

Throw it in your bag the next time you're going out to dinner or a rooftop situation. Pull it out. Use it. Someone will ask what it is. That's the whole point.

Step 05

The Cocktail Rim

Spicy Citrus on a margarita rim. Cinnamon Sugar on an espresso martini or hot toddy. Garlic Butter on the popcorn bowl next to the drinks. The bar is raised. You raised it.

Step 06

The Hosting Hack

Serve in a small bowl on the table. Let guests discover it. You didn't just cook dinner — you curated an experience. That's a Trophy Wife move.
